PlayPin Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summary

PlayPin does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data. It stores only your extension settings: playback speed and whether max quality is enabled.

Data stored

  • Playback speed preference
  • Max quality preference

Where data is stored

Settings are stored with Chrome extension storage. If Chrome sync is enabled, Chrome may sync those settings across your signed-in Chrome browsers under Google's own Chrome sync behavior.

What PlayPin does not collect

  • No browsing history
  • No YouTube watch history
  • No account information
  • No analytics or tracking events
  • No remote server requests from the extension

Permissions

PlayPin asks for storage permission to remember settings and YouTube host access so it can apply those settings on YouTube video pages.

Contact
